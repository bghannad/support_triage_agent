# Category: billing
# Title: Refund policy

Annual plans are refundable on a prorated basis within the first 30 days of purchase, no questions
asked. After 30 days, refunds are only issued for verified billing errors (e.g. duplicate charge,
wrong plan tier selected due to a checkout bug) — not for change-of-mind cancellations. Monthly
plans are not refundable but can be canceled at any time to stop future charges; the customer
retains access until the end of the current billing period. All refund requests should be
processed through the billing team, not by support agents directly.
