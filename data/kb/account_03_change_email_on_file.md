# Category: account_access
# Title: Changing the email address on an account

Email changes are self-serve under Settings > Account > Email, and require confirming the change
via a verification link sent to the new address before it takes effect (the old email stays active
until confirmed, as a security measure against account takeover). If a customer no longer has
access to their old email and can't complete verification, this requires manual identity
verification by support before the change can be forced through — never do this from an inbound
request alone without verifying identity via account details (last 4 of payment method, recent
invoice amount, etc.).
