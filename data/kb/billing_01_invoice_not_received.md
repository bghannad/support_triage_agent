# Category: billing
# Title: Invoice not received

Customers on monthly or annual plans receive invoices automatically by email within 24 hours of
each billing cycle renewal. If an invoice hasn't arrived, first check the spam/junk folder, then
confirm the billing email on file under Settings > Billing > Billing Contact. Invoices can also
be downloaded directly from Settings > Billing > Invoice History at any time, independent of
email delivery. If the invoice is missing from both email and the dashboard, this indicates a
payment processor sync failure and should be escalated to the billing team.
