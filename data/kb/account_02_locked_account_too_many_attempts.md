# Category: account_access
# Title: Account locked after failed login attempts

After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the account locks for 15 minutes as a brute-force
protection measure. This is automatic and cannot be bypassed manually, even by support — the
customer (or support, on their behalf) simply needs to wait out the lock window before retrying.
If lockouts are recurring and the customer is confident they're using the correct password, this
may indicate a saved/cached incorrect password in a browser or password manager, worth asking
about before escalating as a bug.
