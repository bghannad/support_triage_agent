# Title: General criteria for escalating a ticket

Escalate to a human agent (rather than auto-resolving) when: (1) the issue involves billing
disputes above a refund the agent can authorize, (2) there's any indication of security risk or
unauthorized access, (3) the customer explicitly requests a manager/human, (4) confidence in the
retrieved answer is low or the ticket doesn't clearly match a known category, or (5) the request
involves legal/privacy obligations (GDPR, contract terms). When in doubt on a borderline ticket,
escalating is preferred over guessing — a wrong auto-response is more costly than a delayed
human response.
