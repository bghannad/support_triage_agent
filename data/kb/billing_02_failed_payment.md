# Category: billing
# Title: Failed payment / card declined

When a scheduled payment fails, the account enters a 7-day grace period during which all features
remain available. The customer receives an automatic email with a link to update payment details.
Common causes: expired card, insufficient funds, or the issuing bank flagging the charge as
suspicious. After 7 days without a successful retry, the account is downgraded to read-only mode
(data preserved, no new edits) rather than deleted. Full deletion only occurs after 90 days of
continued non-payment, with reminder emails at day 30 and day 60.
