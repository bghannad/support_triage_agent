# Category: account_access
# Title: Resetting a forgotten password

Users can reset their password from the login screen via "Forgot password," which sends a reset
link valid for 1 hour. If the reset email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam and
confirm the account's registered email under Settings > Account (support can look this up by
account ID if the customer can't log in to check). For accounts with SSO enabled (Google/Microsoft
login), there is no separate password — the reset flow does not apply, and the customer should be
directed to their SSO provider's account recovery instead.
