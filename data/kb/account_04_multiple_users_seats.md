# Category: account_access
# Title: Adding or removing team members (seats)

Team/workspace admins can invite new members from Settings > Team > Invite, consuming one seat
per active member. Removing a member frees their seat immediately but does not delete their
historical activity/comments — those remain attributed to their (now removed) account. Seat count
changes are billed on the next cycle, not immediately, except for Enterprise plans which are
billed via a fixed annual seat block agreed with sales.
