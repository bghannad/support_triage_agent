# Category: technical
# Title: Exporting account data

Full account data export (all projects, files, and comments) can be requested from Settings >
Account > Export Data, delivered as a downloadable zip within 24 hours via email link (link
expires after 7 days). This is self-serve and does not require support intervention under normal
circumstances. GDPR-related data requests (a formal "right to access" request from an EU customer)
follow a separate, faster-SLA legal process — these should always be escalated, not handled as a
routine export request.
