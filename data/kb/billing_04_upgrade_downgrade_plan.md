# Category: billing
# Title: Upgrading or downgrading a plan

Plan changes take effect immediately for upgrades (customer is charged a prorated difference for
the remainder of the current cycle) and at the next renewal date for downgrades (no partial
refund for the difference). Switching from monthly to annual billing recalculates the renewal
date to the day of the switch. Enterprise-tier changes require a sales rep and cannot be
self-served from the dashboard — route these to the Enterprise Accounts team.
