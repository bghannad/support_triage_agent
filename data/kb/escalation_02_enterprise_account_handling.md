# Category: escalation_policy
# Title: Enterprise account handling

Any ticket from an account flagged Enterprise-tier should be routed to the dedicated Enterprise
Support queue rather than handled through general triage, regardless of how routine the question
appears — Enterprise contracts include SLA commitments (e.g. 1-hour first response) that the
general queue isn't staffed to guarantee. This applies even to simple questions like plan changes
or seat counts, which for self-serve accounts are fully automatable.
